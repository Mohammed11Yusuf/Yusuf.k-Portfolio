01 // About

From silicon to spectrum

I design the layer where electrons become information.

I'm an Electronics & Telecommunication engineering student fascinated by everything between the antenna and the application layer. Bare-metal firmware, PCB layout, RF chains, DSP pipelines: if it involves a signal, I want to understand it, build it, and push it further.

Currently deep into embedded systems, RF design, digital signal processing and IoT. I ship real hardware, document the process, and treat every datasheet like a novel.

Off the bench you'll find me hunting satellites with an SDR, reverse-engineering consumer gadgets, or teaching juniors how to not blow up a MOSFET.
