01 — PROFILE

Hardware curiosity with a software mindset.

I enjoy understanding the complete path from a physical signal to meaningful data, from circuit and sensor selection to firmware, communication and visualization.

My work lives close to the hardware. I work with microcontrollers such as the ESP32-S3 and Arduino platforms, building systems that interact with sensors, actuators and communication protocols.

I am developing practical knowledge in embedded C/C++, Python automation, IoT communication and electronics prototyping. I also have basic experience with Java and web technologies for presenting or controlling connected systems.

My goal is to become an engineer who can move comfortably between schematics, firmware, debugging tools, data and user-facing applications.
